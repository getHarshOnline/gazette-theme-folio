## For

Folio is Post-AGI Economics’ world for systemic inquiry. Its accepted opening starts on quiet mineral stock and reveals the supplied film through a fine dither aperture, holding the focal point above the detail divider while two premise lines arrive in reading order. Envelope: Institutional.

An institutional publication of systemic inquiry: essays, formal working papers where authored, notebooks and models, project landings, and indexes. Dominant tasks: follow an economic or institutional argument, examine a model, or read and cite a formal document. Number, version and access belong to real papers, not to every piece. Institutional seriousness dominates; its imagery relates ordinary activity to larger arrangements without treating scale as evidence.

- **Registers:** stock almost everywhere; anodised for code in notebooks and the command surface; emulsion for authored imagery and bounded charts; phosphor for held state.

- **Decay:** plain and measured, linear back to rest.

## Why it has this shape

**Let the opening rest before it reveals.** Quiet mineral stock, premise, details, divider and research action make a complete opening before playback. The opening film appears through the aperture; a foreground film remains an explicit optional selection. Sequential premise lines preserve reading order, and local contrast supports copy without adding scenic shadow to the primary action.

**Use a wider view to open inquiry.** A change of scale can connect ordinary activity with its setting. It supplies no economic mechanism, forecast or inevitable progression. Scholarly reading keeps its own still surface.

**Give Paper treatment to actual papers.** Number, version, abstract, citation and access need supplied formal material. Projects keep Product, programme accounts Sequence and essays Reading; a route cannot invent a paper’s institutional address.

**Keep parallel cases equal.** Both conditional cases and their common criteria remain visible together, without ranking, scores or implied progression. Question, premise, context and conclusion keep explicit roles even across several paragraphs; position does not assign meaning.

**Share anatomy while keeping scholarly expression.** Base owns navigation, reading tools, project and service frames, bookmarks and article rows. Folio supplies type and mineral material. Narrative comparison sheets use the shared contour; analytical rows and any future formal paper notches serve their own meaning.
